Lovebombing INC

A community, a show and an app

I built Lovebombing INC from a small group chat into a community across Discord, Telegram, X Spaces, livestreams and IRL events, and kept it active until May 2026.

  1. The group chat

    It started small and grew from the ground up, with light moderation and a lot of trust.

  2. X Spaces

    While I was working at Fluent, I started hosting X Spaces on markets, culture and community, plus full moon and equinox ritual Spaces.

  3. The show

    The Spaces grew into a weekly livestream interview show, simulcast to X, YouTube, Twitch and Instagram. I kept both going after I left Fluent, until April 2026.

  4. The app

    Then I started building Lovebombing, an app for handmade digital love notes.

Lovebombing

Lovebombing

Handmade digital love notes, in the lineage of Ray Johnson’s mail art. You pick a frame, collage images, clips and stickers, and build a beat on a small sequencer. The making is recorded as a timelapse. Each receiver adds a layer and sends it on. The chain is the work.

Built with:
Next.js, Fabric.js, ffmpeg in the browser, Supabase
Made with:
Claude Code and Figma
Also:
An on-chain edition on Stellar, with tips and Folded Letter, an encrypted exquisite-corpse drawing game.

How I work

Good content starts with paying attention. I watch what people react to, then build the post, the clip or the room around it.

OBSRestreamCapCutDescriptCanvaAdobe FireflyFigmaClaude CodeSony ZV-E10DJI Osmo Pocket 3Elgato Cam Link 4K
  1. 1
    Live on the timeline

    I follow conversations as they happen and notice what people actually care about.

  2. 2
    Lead with the idea

    Every post, clip and thread opens on the thing that matters, with no warm-up in front.

  3. 3
    Write like a person

    Copy that sounds like someone on the timeline, not like a brand account.

  4. 4
    Fit each platform

    X, Reels, YouTube and Spotify each get their own cut, format and caption.

  5. 5
    Build the room

    Spaces, group chats and IRL events turn an audience into regulars.
