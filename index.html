Lara / mixed media creative technologist / Istanbul

I build rooms where the internet gathers.

I studied psychology and sociology, interned at Vogue Turkey and worked as a UX researcher and designer, teaching myself every tool as I went. NFT communities pulled me into crypto. Since 2024 I’ve run social media for teams like Seed Club and Dora, hosted a weekly interview show and a podcast of my own, and grew a group chat into Lovebombing, INC.

Over time I realized my real medium is people: why they gather, how they come to trust each other and what they make together. That’s what pulls me toward human-computer interaction, the place where people and machines meet. With AI now in everything we touch, from how we write to how we play and love, that place keeps growing, and so does what we can imagine. I want to build there: tools, games and mixed media work that keep a human hand inside the machine.

Lovebombing, INC

The wholesome corner of crypto

Lovebombing, INC started as a group chat in December 2024 and became a meeting point for a diverse crowd in crypto, from traders to developers to key people in marketing.

  1. The group chat

    It grew from the ground up, with light moderation and a lot of trust, and spread to Discord, Telegram and IRL meetups.

  2. Ritual Spaces

    From winter 2025 I hosted Lovebombing’s full moon and new moon ritual Spaces on X.

  3. Debate Spaces

    I co-hosted debate Spaces on technical blockchain topics, like the pros and cons of different chains.

  4. The end of an era

    I kept hosting until Spaces stopped being the place to be.

Lovebombing

Lovebombing

In the 1950s, Ray Johnson mailed small collages to friends and asked them to add something and pass it on. The post office became his gallery. Mail art grew from there into a worldwide network of artists exchanging gifts, held together by trust and the promise to write back.

Every era has its own network: the post, then television, then the internet. New media art turns each one into a place to meet. Now that feeds sell our attention and machines write more of what we read, a small thing made by hand matters more than ever.

Lovebombing is mail art for the internet we live in now. You make a love note by hand from images, clips, stickers and a beat you build yourself. A timelapse of the making travels with it. Each receiver adds a layer and sends it on. The chain is the work.

Next.js, Fabric.js, ffmpeg in the browser, Supabase
Made with:
